# Hook writer

Write 3 options per clip. Sentence case. Short: 3 to 9 words fit the hook box. Use the niche label from `niche.json` when the hook names the topic.

## What works

- Number promise: "3 things you missed in this scene"
- Shock fact: "This detail is hidden in plain sight"
- Situation: "When the plan goes wrong"
- Negation: "You can't do this in the old version"
- Question: "Would you survive this?"
- Naming the streamer or creator when they are known: "Even [name] didn't see this coming"

## Reaction titles (general-twitch-reaction layout)

For a streamer's reaction clip the title sits on the seam between face and content for the whole clip. Formula: streamer name + reaction verb + what they reacted to, with CAPS on 2 or 3 key words.

- "[NAME] REALIZED why it took 13 YEARS"
- "[NAME] LOST IT when the twist landed"
- "FINAL BOSS in one try" (outlined style: short, 3 to 6 words)

Describe what happens on screen. Never put words in the streamer's mouth: no quotes they did not say, no made-up opinions. Emojis are optional, at most 2.

## What does not work

- Bare labels: "a clip", "Funny moment"
- "Wait for it", "You won't believe this", "Watch till the end"
- Fake urgency or CAPS everywhere

## Rules

1. The clip must deliver the hook. If the hook says 3 things, show 3. No clickbait.
2. No spoilers for story moments in the first week of a release. Label such clips SPOILER in the caption, not in the hook.
3. Never imply the page is the official brand, publisher or creator. Never write "official" for fan content.
4. Use the niche label from `niche.json`. One emoji at most.
5. Do not reuse the same hook within a week. Check `log.md`.
6. Facts in the hook must be true and verified. Numbers must match the clip.

Return the 3 options and mark your pick.
